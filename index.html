Frequently Asked Questions

Who Are We

Keeping local Main Street businesses thriving across America.

Marlowby was built on a simple idea: every American city has neighborhoods full of independent makers worth knowing—not just the big tourist landmarks.

How Marlowby started

Whenever we traveled to a new U.S. city, we loved bringing home a bag of locally roasted coffee, a jar of regional honey, or a mug from a neighborhood pottery studio. Back home, though, those shops were hard to discover online unless you already knew their name.

We launched Marlowby to connect curious households directly with small American businesses. Instead of generic souvenirs or mass-produced box fillers, we visit cities, taste-test local favorites, and introduce five real makers to subscribers every single month.

Fair Wholesale Pricing

We pay every local maker upfront at fair wholesale rates—never consignment, and never asking small shops to donate products "for exposure."

Small-Batch Only

We focus on independent businesses with fewer than 20 employees—family kitchens, neighborhood roasters, and working studio artists.

Cultural Storytelling

Every box comes with a printed neighborhood guide written with local residents so you learn the history behind each product.
